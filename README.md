Midnight Vault Heist — a top-down stealth heist game. You are a thief in a museum after midnight: sneak past three patrolling guards and their yellow vision cones, time the sweeps of three red security cameras, steal all three loot stashes (two display cases and the vault gold, $8,000 in all), and slip out the green EXIT door before the alarm meter hits 100%. Move with WASD or arrows, hold Shift to sneak, press E to grab loot, and click to throw a coin that lures a guard away to investigate. Three routes cross the gallery — the open middle, the crate maze, and the narrow corridor — and a five-minute clock keeps the tension up. Clean getaways pay off the planning; getting caught ends the night.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and press Enter to start the heist.
